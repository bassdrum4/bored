How to interpret this test
A success requires document.pointerLockElement to become non-null. A browser may reject with SecurityError, NotAllowedError, or emit pointerlockerror without a useful message. Fullscreen alone is not pointer lock. Canvas, synthetic clicks, postMessage, and adding permissions to a child iframe cannot override a restrictive outer sandbox. Key-trigger tests require you to focus the page and press the specified key.
