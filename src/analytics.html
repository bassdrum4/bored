Coverage and measurement
Collection begins with this release. Old scores cannot reconstruct past play time. Launches include retries; a session ends on restart, switching games, closing, leaving the page or changing account. “No end received” can mean still playing, a crash, network loss or a closed browser, not proven abandonment. Sessions crossing the selected range are partial. Refresh is manual. Charts use Detroit time; date filters use UTC. Guest IDs change each launch. No cross-device guest matching is attempted.
Client-reported telemetry is useful for product decisions, not anti-cheat or proof of attendance. Opt-outs, blocked requests and outages reduce coverage. Retries are held in memory for this page only. Reports read at most 20,000 events per request and explicitly report a limit; narrow the date range when reached.
