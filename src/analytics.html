Coverage and measurement
Collection begins with this release. Old scores cannot reconstruct past play time. Launches include retries; a session ends on restart, switching games, closing, leaving the page or changing account. “No end received” can mean still playing, a crash, network loss or a closed browser, not proven abandonment. Sessions crossing the selected range are partial. Refresh is manual. Charts use Detroit time; date filters use UTC. Guest browser IDs are persistent only from the new tracking release onward, and can be erased by opting out. They cannot identify people across devices or private browsing.
Client-reported telemetry is useful for product decisions, not anti-cheat or proof of attendance. Opt-outs, blocked requests and outages reduce coverage. Retries are held in memory for this page only. Reports read at most 20,000 events per request and explicitly report a limit; narrow the date range when reached.
