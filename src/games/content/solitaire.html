How to play

Classic Klondike, draw three.

  1. 1Build the four foundations from Ace to King by suit.
  2. 2Build tableau piles in descending order and alternating colors.
  3. 3Only a King can fill an empty tableau slot.
  4. 4Draw three cards from the stock. When the stock is empty, recycle the waste.
  5. 5Click or drag cards to move them. Undo restores the exact previous state.
  6. 6Win when all four foundations reach the King.
