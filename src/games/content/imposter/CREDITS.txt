  map.jpg        Assets/Maps/map2.png, halved to 2896x1584
  mini.png       Assets/Maps/mini_map.PNG, scaled to 1024 wide

  ui-*.png       Assets/Images/UI icons (kill, sabotage, emergency, lights, map)
  task-*.png     Assets/Images/Tasks/* task windows (wiring, garbage, wifi,

  wire-*.png lever-*.png gas-can.png divert-lever.png wifi-lever.png wifi-on.png

  starship.png   Assets/Images/Tasks/Clear Asteroids/starship2.png, 300 wide

The rules are a port of that repo's Python (game.py, sprites.py, map.tmx):
ship size, walk speed, wall rectangles, vent positions, the eight mission
trigger points and their screens, kill/sabotage/meeting timers. The netcode,
host election and room handling are this game's own.
