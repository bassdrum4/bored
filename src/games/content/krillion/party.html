        How to play

        • Answer the category before the puffer explodes on you.
        • Rarer answers fill the meter. 600 points buys an extra heart.
        • You cannot reuse an answer for the current category.
        • The fuse is shared: whatever is left when you pass is what the next diver inherits, with an 8-second floor.
        • You start with 2 hearts and can hold 5. Lose them all and you watch. Last diver floating wins.

        Rated rooms move a rating that starts at 1200; quick play changes nothing.
