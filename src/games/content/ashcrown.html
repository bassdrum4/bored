Civ Lite

Pick a civ, found cities, research a tech tree, and fight over a random map. Pass one device around, add CPU players if you're short a friend, and capture every capital or lead on score when the turns run out.

Everyone starts with a settler and a warrior, at peace. No two players share a civ. Space ends your turn, N jumps to the next unit that needs orders, T opens the tech tree, and H opens the rules.
