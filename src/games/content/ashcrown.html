Ashcrown

The old crown broke, and six banners rose in the vale it left behind. Found cities, work the land, learn what the ruins kept, and be the last banner standing.

How it plays. Click a banner, then a hex. Cities train a unit or raise a building once per turn; technologies cost insight. Claim neutral keeps and found new cities to grow. Win by taking every rival capital, or by raising the Grand Wonder. If nobody has won when the turn limit falls, the highest renown carries the vale.
