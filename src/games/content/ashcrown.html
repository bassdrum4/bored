Civ Lite

Pick a civ, found cities, research a tech tree, and fight over a random map. Pass one device around for up to 8 players, fill the table with CPUs, and win by taking every capital, by science, or on score when the turns run out.

Win by Score wins when the turn limit hits.

Everyone starts with a settler and a warrior, at peace. No two players share a civ. Space ends your turn, N jumps to the next unit that needs orders, T opens the tech tree, R opens your realm, and H opens the rules.
