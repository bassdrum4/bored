Civ Lite

Found cities, grow them, and fight over a random map. Pass one device around, add CPU players if you're short a friend, and capture every capital or lead on score when the turns run out.

Everyone starts with a settler and a warrior. The map is new every game. Space ends your turn, N jumps to the next unit that needs orders, and H opens the rules.
