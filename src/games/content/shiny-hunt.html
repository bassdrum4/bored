how to play
  • Type one Pokémon and press Enter. Small typos are fine; it shows who it thinks you mean.
  • If the name isn't a Pokémon, nothing happens, so keep typing. If it's a real Pokémon that doesn't fit, that prompt scores 0.
  • Rarity compares your answer with every Pokémon that fits, using how well known each one is (the 2019 r/pokemon favourite poll, plus estimates for Gens VIII and IX).
  • Regional forms, Megas and Gigantamax forms count as their own Pokémon with their own types. Their generation is the original Pokémon's.
  • Leaving or refreshing during a daily prompt counts as running out of time.
