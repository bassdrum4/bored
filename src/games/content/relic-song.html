Tap to calibrate

A click plays every half second. Tap any key on each click. After 16 taps the offset is set.

Keyboard

On the moving line, where your hand is on the keyboard is where the note is on screen. IN and AT have 4 regions; HD 2 (the left and right hand); EZ takes any of these keys or Space.
